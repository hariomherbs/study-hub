Deep Focus

01. Study Table Ko Saaf Rakhein

Padhai shuru karne se pehle apni desk par sirf wahi kitab aur copy rakhein jo abhi padhni hai. Bikhra hua saman dhyan bhatkata hai.

Deep Focus

02. Roz Ka Chhota Target Banayein

"Mujhe poori kitab padhni hai" ki jagah "Mujhe aaj sirf 2 chapters khatam karne hain" ka target rakhein. Isse stress kam hota hai.

Sharp Memory

03. Feynman Technique (Doosron Ko Samjhayein)

Jo topic aapne padha hai, use aise samjhayein jaise aap kisi chhote bachhe ko padha rahe hon. Jahan aap atkenge, wahi topic dobara padhein.

Deep Focus

04. Mushkil Subject Pehle Padhein

Jab dimag sabse zyada fresh ho (jaise subah ke waqt), tab sabse mushkil subject padhein. Aasan kaam shaam ke liye chhod dein.

Deep Focus

05. Multitasking Bilkul Na Karein

Ek waqt par ek hi kaam karein. Gaane sunte hue ya phone check karte hue padhne se yaad rakhne ki shamta aadhi reh jati hai.

Fast Revision

06. Short Notes Banayein

Lambe paragraphs ratne ke bajaye bullet points aur diagrams bana kar notes likhein, taaki exam ke waqt revision tez ho sake.

Fast Revision

07. Spaced Repetition (Samay-Samay Par Revision)

Aaj jo padha hai, use 24 ghante baad, phir 7 din baad aur phir 1 mahine baad zaroor revise karein. Isse padha hua kabhi nahi bhoolta.

Sharp Memory

08. Raat Ko Sone Se Pehle Revision

Sone se 15 minute pehle din bhar padhe gaye main points ko ek baar dimag mein dohra lein—neend ke dauran dimag unhe pakka kar leta hai.
